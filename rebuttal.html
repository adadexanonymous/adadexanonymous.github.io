Reference-plus-Noise vs. Random Goals

Both policies combine motion tracking with goal-reaching training (50/50 TrackEnv/GenEnv). They differ only in how goals are sampled in the goal-reaching environments.

Left — Reference-plus-noise (AdaDex): goals are sampled by perturbing future poses from the demonstration.
Right — Random goals: object goals are sampled independently of the demonstrated trajectory.

Each policy with its native training goal sampler

Each policy receives the type of goals used during its training. The left follows perturbed demonstration goals; the right follows independently sampled random goals. This shows the behavior each training objective encourages.
